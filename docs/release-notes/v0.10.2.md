## 新功能 · What's new

- 🔍 **搜索框底部一排搜索引擎**：输入了词，点一个引擎就在那个网站搜这个词；什么都没输入就打开它的网站。谷歌学术、百度、arXiv 开箱即用，点击后启动器照常收起。
  **A row of search engines at the foot of the box**: with words typed, a click searches them at that site; with none, it opens the site. 谷歌学术, 百度 and arXiv are there to begin with, and the launcher tucks itself away as usual.
- ✏️ **引擎自己加、自己改**：设置 › 网页搜索 › 搜索框底部的引擎 里可以添加、编辑、删除：起个名字，网址里用 `%s` 代替要搜的文字（没有 `%s` 就是点了直接打开那一页）。
  **Engines of your own**: under Settings › Web search › Footer engines they are added, edited and removed — a name, and an address with `%s` where the words go (without `%s` a click just opens that page).
- ⠿ **拖一拖就能调顺序**：设置里每个引擎行左侧有一个抓手，按住上下拖，别的行让路，松手就记住，底部按钮的排布跟着变。
  **Drag them into order**: a grip sits at each engine row's left; drag it up or down, the rows make way, and on release the order is kept — the buttons at the foot follow it.
- ⚙ **右下角的设置按钮**：搜索框底部一排的最右边多了一个齿轮，点开就是设置，不用再从托盘找。
  **A settings button at the corner**: a gear at the right end of the footer opens the settings, no need to look for the tray icon.

## 修复 · Fixed

- 🗂 **0.10.0 写的旧索引快照不再被读串**：0.10.1 给排除规则加了“排除扩展名”，但没升快照的版本号——0.10.0 留下的快照会被按新格式读，低优先级路径落进扩展名里、后面的内容解析失败，索引只好整个重建。现在按版本区分读取，旧快照照常生效。
  **An old snapshot is read right again**: 0.10.1 added excluded extensions to the crawl rules without raising the snapshot's version, so a snapshot left by 0.10.0 was read one list off — low-priority paths landed in the extensions, the rest did not parse, and the index rebuilt itself. The reader now tells the versions apart.
- 🟦 **页面上不再悬着空的小方块**：引擎列表变化或底部收起之后，提示气泡偶尔留在原处、内容成了空串，看着像一个多余的黑色小块。现在索引失效就复位，气泡只在该说话的时候出现。
  **No more lingering empty block**: after the engines changed or the footer hid, a tooltip could stay where it was with nothing to say — an odd little dark square. The hovered index resets now, and a tooltip only shows while it has something to say.
